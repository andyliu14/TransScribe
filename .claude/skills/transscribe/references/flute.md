# Concert flute

Write treble clef at concert pitch. C4 is the usual floor with a C footjoint. Verify a B footjoint for B3. The low register can disappear under sustained middle-register strings. Thin the accompaniment at a low entrance or move the phrase where its attack can carry.

Make long slurred runs lead toward a release. A sustained soft ending can still consume substantial air. Time the phrase in seconds at the slowest intended tempo and leave a breath before its repeat. Check the fingering and attack on high repeated notes with the intended player.
