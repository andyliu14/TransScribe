# Double bass

For standard four-string orchestral tuning the open strings sound E1, A1, D2, G2. A sounding E1 appears as E2 in the bass-clef part. Check both the extracted notation and concert playback. A low C needs an extension, different tuning or another instrument.

Build a bass line around arrivals, releases and changes of harmony. A walking quarter-note approach can connect roots, while a sustained bowed pedal can leave rhythmic space for the upper parts. Fast low repeated notes need clear releases to speak.

Test adjacent notes as a shift at the chosen tempo. Check multiple stops on separate strings and allow time for pizzicato/arco changes. In a fast cello/bass exchange, try giving the bass the rhythmic skeleton and account for each removed inner tone.
