# Piano

Distribute a grand-staff texture by hand and gesture. A held bass, inner pulse and treble tune may be three independent voices. Show melody stems and releases clearly when the same hand shares an accompaniment.

Test a chord from the hand position left by the preceding event. An octave in isolation says little about landing an octave after a fast leap. Redistribute the inner pitch or roll a chord when the melody can tolerate the delay. Mark pedal changes against the harmonic rhythm and test whether the written durations work without pedal at a harmony change.

In a trio reduction, audit the bass attack, the tune and any inner suspension separately. Passing the tune to sax can free cello to resolve the suspension.
