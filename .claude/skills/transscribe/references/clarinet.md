# B-flat clarinet

Written C4 sounds B♭3. The ordinary low written E3 is sounding D3. Distinguish this instrument from A clarinet and bass clarinet. Keep register labels and all pitch examples in the same pitch convention on a review sheet.

The low chalumeau register suits a dark answering phrase. Moving from written throat B♭4 to clarion B4 crosses the break and changes many fingers. A sustained crossing can be expressive; repeated fast oscillations there deserve a fingering check. Try moving the turn within a phrase or handing its answer to another instrument.

Read the line at tempo with its slurs and accents. Reserve enough of the preceding rest for a clean quiet entrance and check the first breath after any repeated section.
