# Six-string guitar

Standard tuning sounds E2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4. Conventional treble notation reads an octave higher. Establish tuning and capo before checking shapes. Adjacent pitches may lie on the same string and need separate attacks.

Try a chord as a simultaneous fretted shape, including the shift from the previous grip. An open string can ring over a position change, but it can also blur the next chord. Indicate where that resonance should end. A picked arpeggio can preserve a bass pedal and treble melody when a blocked chord interrupts the line.

For a sung or wind melody doubled on guitar, test how long a high fretted note sustains after its attack. Repeat, tremolo or hand the sustain to another voice when the shape of the phrase depends on it.
