# Cello

The open strings ascend C2, G2, D3, A3 at concert pitch. Bass clef covers the low line. Tenor and treble help when a phrase stays high. Read an extracted page through each clef change and check the next system after a rest.

A melody can climb on one string for continuity of color, or cross to an open string for resonance. Give a rising countermelody room above sustained bass. Under a soft sax entrance, shorten a thick low double stop or pass its inner tone to another voice.

Trace string and position choices through the whole gesture. Two pitches playable in isolation can need the same string. A slur grouping several string crossings also consumes bow. Test the shift back down and the bow direction available for the next accent.
